00 Executive Summary

This document outlines the complete engineering workflow undertaken to build the interactive 3D Industrial Smart Campus and Energy Management System (EMS). The project transforms raw electrical consumption telemetry and contract demand parameters from a 12-division industrial matrix (Image 1) into an interactive, real-time 3D digital twin platform (Image 2) with animated power conduits, dynamic camera controls, and analytical diagnostic drawers.

02 Phase 2: Tech Stack & System Architecture

Core 3D Engine: Three.js (r128)
High-performance WebGL 2.0 renderer capable of rendering multi-mesh scenes, PBR materials, soft directional shadows, and bloom post-processing at steady 60 FPS across desktop and mobile.
Navigation: OrbitControls
Constrained orbital camera system supporting smooth 360° rotational inspection, responsive zooming, and clamped polar angles to prevent subterranean camera clipping.
Animation: Tween.js
Interpolates camera positions and look-at target vectors using cubic-out easing curves for cinematic fly-to transitions when selecting industrial buildings.
Visualization: Chart.js
Lightweight canvas charting framework for rendering historical monthly consumption profiles, load fluctuations, and equipment telemetry in the unit inspection modal.

03 Phase 3: Spatial Coordinate Modeling & Data Structuring

The industrial campus follows a radial layout centered on the 33 kV substation. In js/data.js, each unit was assigned a radial distance ($r$) and an angular orientation ($\theta$) matching the circular layout of Image 2:

Spatial Transformation Formula:
$$X = r \cdot \cos(\theta_{\text{rad}}), \quad Z = -r \cdot \sin(\theta_{\text{rad}}), \quad \text{Rotation}_Y = -\theta_{\text{rad}} + \frac{\pi}{2}$$ This formula places each factory along the ring road and automatically orients the factory's front loading apron towards the center of the campus.

04 Phase 4: 3D Scene Engineering (`js/scene3d.js`)

1. Central 33 kV Energy Hub Substation

  • Energy Reactor Core: Modeled with an illuminated neon green cylinder (#00e676) housed inside a transparent refractive glass cylinder.
  • Dual Gyro Rings: Two counter-rotating holographic torus rings that spin on the X/Y axes during runtime.
  • Transformer Banks: Four 3D step-down oil transformer tanks with cooling radiators and high-voltage bushing insulators.
  • Lattice Gantry Towers: Structural lattice pylons, crossbeams, and busbar insulator strings.

2. 12 Parametric Manufacturing Units

  • Industrial Warehouses: Modeled with pitched steel roof frames, industrial cladding, roll-up loading bays, and glass clerestory windows.
  • Photovoltaic Solar Arrays: High-density photovoltaic panels mounted across the roofs, matching Image 2's clean-energy campus design.
  • Distinct Structural Props: High-tonnage gantry cranes for Units 1 & 2; gas storage tanks for Unit 3; exhaust chimneys for Units 4 & 9; prototype transmission towers for Unit 5; autoclave curing chambers for Unit 11.

3. Dynamic Glowing Energy Conduit Beams

  • Constructed 3D QuadraticBezierCurve3 splines arching upward from the central hub into each factory's step-down transformer box.
  • Generated animated particle spheres that traverse the curves continuously, producing a live visual energy pulse.

4. Campus Landscaping & Perimeter Forest

  • Concentric asphalt road rings (inner loop and outer highway) and 12 radial connection streets.
  • Surrounding forest of 380+ procedural 3D conifers and deciduous trees with randomized green foliage shaders.

05 Phase 5: 3D-to-2D Projected UI HUD & Marker Occlusion

Floating callout cards were overlaid on the 3D scene using screen coordinate projection:

  • World-to-Screen Mapping: In each render frame, building 3D coordinates $(X, Y, Z)$ are projected using vector.project(camera) to calculate 2D viewport coordinates $(x, y)$.
  • Frustum Occlusion Elimination: A vector dot product check with the camera's forward vector ($\vec{d}_{\text{cam}} \cdot \vec{d}_{\text{obj}} \le 0$) hides labels when their building moves behind the camera viewpoint, preventing UI inversion.

06 Phase 6: Interactive Dashboard & Diagnostics (`js/app.js`)

  • Header Telemetry: Live aggregated metrics (10,394 kW Load, 3,84,791 kWh Consumption, 0.97 Avg PF, Live Date/Clock).
  • Right Analytics Panel: Animated live energy flow schematic (`Grid <-> Hub <-> Units`), environment impact (`21.35 Tonnes CO₂ Saved`), and status flags.
  • Diagnostic Inspection Drawer: Clicking any unit flies the 3D camera to that building and opens a modal with equipment load ratings and historical consumption charts.
  • Simulation Ticker: Simulates realistic $\pm 1.5\%$ micro-fluctuations in electrical loads to provide a living digital twin experience.
