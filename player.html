Why does a dangling pointer cause a segmentation fault?
Asked by Ayush Sharma • IIT Roorkee
Prof. Rajesh: When memory is freed using free(ptr), the OS reclaims the page or marks it unallocated. Dereferencing ptr afterward accesses unmapped memory, causing a SIGSEGV.
